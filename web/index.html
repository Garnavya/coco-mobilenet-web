Architectural Dissection: Where Did Track A Fall Short?

Mathematical and methodological breakdown of the multi-task capacity collapse.

01. Spatial Vector Trap

Global Pooling Erases 2D Geometry

In `hydra/model_mono.py`, the shared backbone applies `nn.AdaptiveAvgPool2d(1)` to the 960-channel feature map, collapsing spatial resolution ($8 \times 8$) into a single 1D vector of 960 numbers. Spatial coordinates are averaged out. Expecting a dense linear regressor to reconstruct 17 precise $(x, y)$ joint locations across the canvas from a non-spatial summary is mathematically underdetermined, causing joints to collapse around the centroid of mass ($OKS \approx 0.001$).

02. Cropping Discrepancy

Top-Down Crop vs. Cluttered Canvas Guessing

Track B evaluated pose using Oracle Ground-Truth Cropping: each human was pre-cropped, centered, and scaled to $256 \times 256$, simplifying the task to local limb localization. Conversely, Track A was forced to detect, isolate, and locate joints across the entire cluttered $256 \times 256$ full-image canvas without spatial attention or localized cropping.

03. Gradient Competition

Cross-Entropy Norm Domination

Cross-Entropy loss across a 10,192-word vocabulary naturally produces high scalar values ($\approx 9.16$ at initialization), generating massive gradient norms. A static weighting formula $(50 \times \text{Box}) + (50 \times \text{Pose}) + (1 \times \text{Cap})$ balances early epochs, but fails as tasks converge at non-linear rates. Dynamic projection (PCGrad / GradNorm) is needed.

04. Backbone Fine-Tuning

Backbone Freezing vs. End-to-End Thrashing

Track B captioning kept the MobileNetV3 backbone frozen with ImageNet pre-trained weights, preserving visual token separation. Track A updated the entire backbone simultaneously at $lr=0.001$ under 3 contradictory objectives, degrading pre-trained feature discriminability.

Academic Metrics Encyclopedia

Formal mathematical formulas, interpretation rules, and failure signatures.

mAP @ 0.50:0.95 (Mean Average Precision)

Detection

Averages the area under the Precision-Recall curve across 10 IoU overlap thresholds ($0.50, 0.55, \dots, 0.95$). Evaluates localization accuracy and multi-instance recall across varied bounding box sizes.

$$\text{mAP} = \frac{1}{|T|} \sum_{t \in T} \int_{0}^{1} p_t(r) \, dr$$

OKS AP (Object Keypoint Similarity)

Pose

Measures Euclidean distance between predicted and ground-truth keypoints normalized by subject scale ($s$) and joint variance ($\sigma_i$). Scale normalization prevents large humans from dominating error penalties.

$$\text{OKS} = \frac{\sum_i \exp\left(-\frac{d_i^2}{2 s^2 \sigma_i^2}\right) \delta(v_i > 0)}{\sum_i \delta(v_i > 0)}$$

CIDEr (Consensus-based Image Description)

Semantics

Measures human consensus using Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF). Rewards rare, specific descriptive words and ignores filler stop words ("a", "the", "is").

$$\text{CIDEr}_n(c_i, S_i) = \frac{1}{m} \sum_{j} \frac{g^n(c_i) \cdot g^n(s_{ij})}{\|g^n(c_i)\| \|g^n(s_{ij})\|}$$

BLEU-4 (Bilingual Evaluation Understudy)

Grammar

Counts exact matches of 4-word n-gram sequences against reference captions with brevity penalty ($BP$) to ensure syntax and grammatical fluency are maintained.

$$\text{BLEU-4} = BP \cdot \exp\left(\sum_{n=1}^{4} w_n \log p_n\right)$$
